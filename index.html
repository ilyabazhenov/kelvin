How it works

Set it up once.

Kelvin adds a microphone called “Kelvin Mic” to your Mac. Your call app listens to it, and Kelvin listens to your real mic.

  1. Install KelvinIt asks for your mic once and adds “Kelvin Mic” to the system.
  2. Pick “Kelvin Mic” in your call appIn Zoom, Meet or Teams audio settings. They remember it.
  3. TalkThe lamp lights up ON AIR while an app hears you. “How do I sound” plays back 6 seconds of you, before and after.

Why “Kelvin”

Light has a temperature. So does a voice.

Light colour is measured in kelvins. An office ceiling panel is about 6500K: cold and flat. A lamp at home is 2700K, firelight about 1900K. The light on this page warms the same way as you scroll down.

Questions

Before you download.

How do I remove background noise from my mic on a Mac?

Install Kelvin and choose “Kelvin Mic” as the microphone in your call app. A neural noise suppressor running on your Mac takes out typing, air vents, fans and mains hum before the app hears you.

Does it work in Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams?

Yes. Kelvin Mic is a regular system microphone, so it works in any app that lets you pick a mic: Zoom, Google Meet and Teams in the browser or the desktop app, FaceTime, Slack, Discord and recording apps.

Is Kelvin free?

Yes. Kelvin is free to download and use, with no account and no sign-up.

Does my voice go to a server?

No. All processing runs on your Mac. Kelvin works offline and never sends your audio anywhere.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 and later) on macOS 14 Sonoma or newer. Intel Macs are not supported.

How is it different from Voice Isolation or Krisp?

Like Krisp, Kelvin is a virtual microphone that removes noise for every app at once. macOS Voice Isolation removes noise too, but only where an app offers it. On top of noise removal, Kelvin's Voice keys shape the tone: Clearer, Warmer or Broadcast instead of a thin laptop voice.
